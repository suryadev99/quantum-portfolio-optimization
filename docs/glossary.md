# Glossary

See §3 of [`FEATURE_SPEC.md`](FEATURE_SPEC.md) for the full glossary. Shortcuts:

* **QUBO** – quadratic unconstrained binary optimisation. **Ising** – the same with ±1 spins (Z operators).
* **QAOA** – Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm; **CVaR** – mean of the best α-fraction of sampled energies.
* **Dicke state** – equal superposition of all bitstrings with fixed Hamming weight; **XY mixer** preserves that weight.
* **Cut share** – fraction of covariance mass between clusters (lost by decomposition).
* **ε-band** – set of portfolios within ε of the best objective; **Jaccard distance** – 1 − |A∩B|/|A∪B|.
* **Effective N** – 1/Σwᵢ²; **Sharpe** here = (μᵀw − r_f)/√(wᵀΣw).
